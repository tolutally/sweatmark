## Description
SweatMark keeps your workout log simple, so you can focus on your training.

LOG YOUR NUMBERS
Record sets, reps, and weight. Track cardio time and distance, with optional heart rate and effort notes.

MAKE IT YOUR ROUTINE
Save the exercises you use together as a routine. Preview it before starting, or begin an empty workout and add exercises as you go. Create custom exercises for movements missing from the library.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
Explore monthly and yearly workout totals, time spent training, strength volume, and muscle-group set counts. Open a card for the details, or view progress for an individual exercise.

KEEP YOUR STREAK
Set an editable weekly workout goal and follow your consistency over time.

LOG EVEN WHEN YOU'RE OFFLINE
Workouts and custom exercises save on your device. Pending changes retry syncing to your account when you're connected and the app is running.

Built for keeping track of your exercises and your numbers, with a clean interface that stays out of the way.
